What to include in your first message

Include your arrival and departure dates, group size and the questions where you would like help. You can share your interests, an approximate plan or an organisational difficulty you are already facing.

If you would like in-person companionship, add your preferred days and hours. You do not need a finished itinerary: we can discuss what kind of support would be useful together.

What we agree before confirmation

An individual offer defines the scope of support, service period, contact hours, response expectations and price. Transport, tickets and partner services are identified as separate expenses.

After discussing the price and terms, you decide whether to confirm the offer. If your plans are still developing, tell us before the final agreement so the scope of support matches your actual needs.

Frequently asked questions

Can I contact you before arriving in Armenia?

Yes. Send your dates and needs in advance so we can discuss support options and an individual offer.

What if I do not have a detailed plan yet?

Tell us about your interests and approximate dates. We can use the conversation to clarify your plans and the scope of assistance you need.

Does sending a message confirm the service?

No. We discuss your needs, price and terms first. The service is confirmed by a separate agreement.
