A local contact when Armenia is new to you

ARMMATE is a personal tourist assistance service for independent visitors to Armenia. Your local assistant helps with everyday travel decisions, from choosing where to eat to communicating with a driver, restaurant or hotel.

Think of a personal travel concierge: tell us your interests, pace and budget, and we help you find practical options. You might want a quiet day in Yerevan, a cultural experience or a trip outside the city. You make the choices, while your assistant helps with the next steps.

What online travel assistance can cover

The main service is personal communication over WhatsApp or Telegram during an agreed service period. You can discuss plans before your visit and ask for help with specific situations while you are here.

  • Suggestions for places, cafés, restaurants and cultural activities.
  • Discussing routes and finding taxis or private drivers.
  • Help with restaurant reservations and finding event tickets.
  • Translation and communication with local services.
  • Practical support when hotel arrangements, transport or lost belongings cause difficulties.

Support without a compulsory tour

You do not have to join a group excursion or follow a fixed programme. If you want to visit Garni, for example, your assistant can help discuss the journey, transport and possible stops. If you would like a specialist guide, we can help find that separate service.

Transport, accommodation, tickets and partner services have their own costs, separated from the assistance fee. Online support does not automatically include them. If you would like a local person physically beside you, personal companionship can be discussed separately.

An offer based on your own trip

Share your travel dates, group size, interests and the situations where you would like support. The price depends on your stay, the kind and amount of assistance, and whether you want any in-person companionship.

Before confirmation, we agree the service period, contact hours, response expectations and separate expenses. There are no mandatory packages or automatic price calculations. Your assistant provides practical organisational help and does not replace emergency medical or rescue services.

Frequently asked questions

Is ARMMATE a tour agency?

ARMMATE provides personal local assistance. You can explore Armenia independently and contact your assistant for recommendations and practical organisational support.

Will my assistant be with me in person all the time?

The main service is online assistance. Personal companionship is optional and arranged separately, with the days, hours, activities and price agreed in advance.

Can you help organise an airport transfer?

We can help find and organise suitable transport. The driver or transfer has a separate cost, and availability is checked when making arrangements.

Is assistance available around the clock?

Only if separately agreed. Each offer defines the contact hours, service period and expected scope of support.
