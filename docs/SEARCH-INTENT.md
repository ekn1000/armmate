# Search intent and page ownership

Reviewed on 22 November 2026. This is qualitative intent research based on the owner's service model, the supplied traveller questions, the current website and the Armenia Travel destination resources cited on each guide. No paid keyword-volume data, Search Console query exports or competitor ranking measurements were available. No volumes or ranking opportunities are invented.

Each page family has a complete Armenian and Russian equivalent with reciprocal language links. Existing service routes are retained. There are no separate near-duplicate landing pages for every synonym such as “local support”, “foreign visitor assistance” and “travel planning”. Related terms belong naturally on the primary assistance page and the independent-planning guide.

The destination pages are visitor guidance, not claims that Armmate owns accommodation, transport, sites or attractions. They explain optional assistance and separately agreed accompaniment. They do not invent schedules, ticket prices, safety guarantees or guaranteed access. The six destination guides are different in their decisions, routes and FAQs; names are not substituted into a repeated city template.

## Editorial maintenance

- Revise a guide when the underlying advice or cited source changes; do not change dates merely to simulate freshness.
- Check a source's main content, not just a search snippet, before adding new factual claims.
- Cite official tourism/site information for destination facts and official operators for dates, schedules or admission rules. Current prices and schedules are deliberately not published here.
- Translate substantive changes into all three languages before rebuilding.
- Keep one primary page for each search intent. Link related pages instead of creating competing synonym pages.
- Retain useful standalone advice even if the reader chooses not to buy assistance.
